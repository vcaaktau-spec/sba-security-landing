# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Business owners, commercial property managers, and private homeowners in Aktau and the Mangystau region of Kazakhstan who need highly reliable security installations (CCTV, fire alarms, access control) and 24/7 monitoring.

## Product Purpose
SBA Security exists to deliver professional, fail-safe security systems. The landing page acts as an elite technical command briefing, proving their engineering excellence, reliability, and precision, and encouraging potential clients to request quotes via the interactive system calculator or WhatsApp.

## Brand Personality
Sentinel, Tactical, Prestige. An elite operations dossier that feels reassuringly strict, clean, and mechanically precise.
- **3-word personality**: Sentinel, Tactical, Prestige
- **Emotional goals**: Command-level confidence, relief, professional trust, absolute safety.

## Anti-references
- **Low-cost template websites**: Generic, flat, or gray tables with cheap shadows and padlock stock icons.
- **Dark hacker theme clichés**: Over-stylized dark cyberpunk aesthetics with bright neon cyan lines that distract from actual trust.
- **Generic corporate editorial**: Saturated "cream-sand-beige" agency portfolios with italic serif headlines that feel mismatched for a physical security installation service.

## Design Principles
1. **Swiss Technical Grid**: Use clean off-white canvas with thin tactical dividers, crosshair grid alignments, and monospace labels to evoke engineering blueprints.
2. **Operational Status Highlights**: A clean slate/white layout accented by solid tactical red/crimson indicators representing readiness and active alarm signals.
3. **Live Tactical Monitor**: Hero displays real-time animated status grids, camera feeds status, and diagnostic reports to simulate visual security control.
4. **Heavy Asymmetric Typography**: Clean, wide display geometric sans-serif headings with high contrast weights.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High text-to-background contrast (>= 4.5:1).
- Accessible form controls in the calculator modal.
- Screen reader friendly monospace status labels.
